# ATS-safe structure

Rebuild to this. Plain, single column, conventional headings. Output as .docx.

---

**FIRSTNAME LASTNAME**
Job title or target role
Dubai, UAE · +971 XX XXX XXXX · name@email.com · linkedin.com/in/username
Nationality: [X] · Visa status: [X]

*All of this in the body of the document. Never in the header or footer.*

---

**PROFILE**

Three or four lines. What you do, at what level, in what sector, and the one thing that distinguishes you. No adjectives that anyone could claim.

---

**EXPERIENCE**

**Job Title**
Company Name, City · Mar 2021 to Present

- Achievement with a number in it wherever a number exists
- Scope: who you supported, how many, what budget, which regions
- The thing you owned that nobody else did

**Job Title**
Company Name, City · Jan 2018 to Feb 2021

- As above

*One date format throughout. `Mar 2021` and `03/2021` are both fine. Mixing them is not, because gaps are computed from these.*

---

**EDUCATION**

Qualification, Institution, Year

---

**SKILLS**

Written as text, comma separated, grouped if there are many. Give acronyms and full terms together the first time: Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

Never as a graphic, a rating bar or a five-star scale. None of it survives extraction, and a bar chart of "Excel: 4/5" tells a reader nothing anyway.

---

**LANGUAGES**

English (native) · Arabic (conversational)

---

**Optional, by judgement**

Certifications · Publications · Volunteering

---

## Rules for the rebuild

- One column. No text boxes. No tables except a simple one if it genuinely helps.
- Standard bullets. No custom icons.
- A common font at 10 to 12pt. Nothing decorative.
- No images, no logos, no charts, no photo in the file being submitted to the ATS.
- Two pages. Three at the absolute outside.
- Filename: `firstname-lastname-cv.docx`. No spaces, no brackets, no "final".
- Save under 1MB if a regional board is involved.

## What must not change in a rebuild

Every date, employer, job title, qualification and number stays exactly as the person gave it. Rewriting how something is phrased is help. Adding something they did not claim is writing a false document.
